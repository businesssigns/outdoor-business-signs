Popular Types of Outdoor Business Signs

From classic fascia signs to modern illuminated displays, we offer solutions that suit every building style and marketing goal.

1

Fascia & Storefront Signs

The classic above-entrance sign that anchors your brand identity. Ideal for retail shops, cafes, and offices seeking a clean, professional look.

2

Projecting / Blade Signs

Mounted perpendicular to the building so they can be seen from both directions along the street—perfect for high-traffic pedestrian areas.

3

Pylon & Monument Signs

Freestanding structures that mark entrances to shopping centres, industrial parks, or multi-tenant sites with high visibility from the road.

4

Channel Letter Signs

Three-dimensional individual letters that can be illuminated from within or with halo lighting for a premium, modern appearance.

5

A-Frame & Pavement Signs

Portable sidewalk signs ideal for daily specials, promotions, or temporary messaging outside cafes, salons, and boutique stores.

6

Directional & Wayfinding Signs

Clear, consistent signage that guides customers, deliveries, and staff around larger properties, car parks, and multi-building sites.
